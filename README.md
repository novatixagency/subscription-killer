# Subscription Killer by Novatix

**Stop Burning Money on Subscriptions.**

🔗 **Live:** https://save.novatix.agency

A free, privacy-first subscription waste calculator. Tick the subscriptions you pay for, see exactly how much money you're burning every year, and get 1-click free alternatives — no bank account linking, no signup, no cost. Ever.

## ✨ Features

- **🧮 Interactive waste calculator** — 25+ subscriptions with live monthly/yearly totals and animated count-ups
- **⚠️ Smart overlap detection** — detects when you pay for multiple services in the same category (e.g. 3 music apps) and shows exactly how much you'd save by keeping just one
- **🌍 PK/UK subscriptions** — Jazz, Zong, Tapmad, STARZPLAY, Foodpanda, Daraz, BBC iPlayer, TNT Sports, plus all major US/global services
- **📈 Price hike alerts** — warns you when a service raised its prices recently and how much extra you're now paying per year
- **💡 Annual vs monthly savings** — shows how much you'd save by switching eligible subscriptions to yearly billing
- **🔗 1-click free alternatives** — every paid subscription maps to a free Novatix tool replacement with your personal yearly savings
- **📊 Shock report** — dramatic results view with category breakdowns, 5-year compounding projections, and real-world equivalencies
- **🔗 Viral share** — personalized "Financial Liberation Pass" with X/WhatsApp/LinkedIn share intents and native Web Share API
- **🔒 100% private** — everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded or stored
